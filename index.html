How It Works

📋 Step-by-Step Rental Process

  1. Browse our fleet online and choose your ideal vehicle.
  2. Select your pickup date, time, and preferred location.
  3. Submit your driver's licence and valid national ID for verification.
  4. Complete payment via M-Pesa, credit card, or bank transfer.
  5. Pick up your keys or request airport delivery — and drive away!
