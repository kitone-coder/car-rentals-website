📋 Rental Requirements

  1. Valid Kenyan or International Driver's Licence
  2. National ID or valid passport (original, not copy)
  3. Security deposit — refunded in full on safe return
  4. Minimum age of 23 years at time of booking
  5. Completed booking form and signed rental agreement

💳 Accepted Payments

  • M-Pesa (Paybill & Send Money)
  • Visa / Mastercard credit or debit card
  • Bank transfer (local & international)
  • Cash (KES only, at our office)
