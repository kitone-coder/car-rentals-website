About DriveEasy

Nairobi's most trusted car rental company — serving drivers since 2015.

Our Story

Built for Kenyan Roads

DriveEasy was founded in 2015 with one mission: make car rental simple, transparent, and affordable for every Kenyan. What started with a 5-car fleet in Westlands has grown into one of Nairobi's most trusted rental services with over 50 vehicles.

We serve tourists, business travellers, families, and corporations — delivering vehicles that are always clean, well-maintained, and fully insured.

Every journey matters to us. Whether you're heading to Maasai Mara, the coast, or a morning meeting across the city, DriveEasy is your reliable partner on the road.
